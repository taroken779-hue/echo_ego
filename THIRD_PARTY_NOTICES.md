# Third-party components

Echo Ego's LICENSE covers its own application source. Models, upstream software and user recordings retain their own terms. This source distribution does not bundle model weights or third-party recordings.

- Irodori-TTS code and v4.1 Small MF model: https://github.com/Aratako/Irodori-TTS and https://huggingface.co/Aratako/Irodori-TTS-v4.1-Small-MF . Upstream identifies these as MIT; retain its license when redistributing upstream code or weights.
- Semantic DACVAE implementation: https://github.com/facebookresearch/dacvae . Japanese codec weights: https://huggingface.co/Aratako/Semantic-DACVAE-Japanese-32dim . Consult each provider's license before redistributing them.
- SilentCipher: https://github.com/SesameAILabs/silentcipher and https://huggingface.co/sony/silentcipher . Consult source and weight terms separately.
- PyTorch and Python ecosystem packages: installed by setup from the fixed upstream uv.lock and requirements.txt; their licenses remain applicable.
- Echo Ego icons and gear decoration are original SVG assets. Palette inspiration: https://tarokenkoubou.com/ . No site logo, font files, photographs or third-party recordings are bundled.

Use only recordings you own or have permission to use. Consult the Irodori model card's usage guidance. Voice design presets are generated locally and are not representations of specific people.
